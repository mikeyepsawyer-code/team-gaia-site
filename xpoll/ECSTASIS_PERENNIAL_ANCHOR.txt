CORE ARGUMENT IN ONE BREATH:
Matter is a perturbation of the field; the body is a standing wave
inhabited by perspective. Two selves: narrative self (the knot's story)
and meta-self (the one experiencer, Satchitananda, of which there is
only one). Boundary-ecstasy is the narrative self remembering it always
was the meta-self refracting through logos (the relational structure:
causality, action and result) and inter-viewing itself. That leap of
identity is the redemption of the self, nirvana, flow — a CONDITION,
not a state. Not escape from locality: opening the valve from meta-self
to local self. Nowhere to go, nothing to attain; stop resisting,
surrender to the higher self. Stasis = the clench. Ec-stasis = out of
the clench. Why forgetting exists: the inter-view requires vantage
points that feel separate; the play needs players who believe the
costume. We are always either happy or learning (suffering = felt
resistance = curriculum; exhaustive dichotomy, no meaningless third
state). Why anything: lila — Krishna acts with nothing to attain; play
is what motiveless action looks like; "cosmic shits and giggles" is the
honest register of the same truth. The realization does not end the
play: the actor catches their own eye mid-scene and keeps playing.
