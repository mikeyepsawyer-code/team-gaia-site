[MICHAEL — verbatim]

"The fact that by participating in the projection of life, suffering
and dying is inevitable. The fact that as individuated beings, we are
open to predation by otherness.

[...]

The second golden rule: suffering has a cause, and that cause is
discernible. The cause of suffering is attachment.

In other words, when we're attached to the ideation of nounism, we
are traveling backward in time, caught up in the current of entropy.
We are suffering through self-dissipation.

Third golden rule: Because the cause of suffering is discernible as
attachment, the cessation of suffering is caused by non-attachment.

But here's the rub: it's easy to confuse non-attachment with
detachment.

Attachment is grasping; detachment is attachment by pushing away.
Non-attachment is standing still while the universe flows through
you.

Allowing forms to come and go without getting stuck in a preference
for one form-image, which by its nature, is headed toward the past."
