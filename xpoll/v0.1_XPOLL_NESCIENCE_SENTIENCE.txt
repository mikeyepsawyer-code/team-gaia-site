[CONDENSED — Gemini exchange, paraphrased from the original]

Nescience means a lack of knowledge; its precise antonym is science
or sapience. Insentience means a lack of feeling or awareness; its
precise antonym is sentience. The two are related but not identical.

Argument for treating them as the same state: perceiving a sensation
requires the cognitive data of that sensation, so an entity with zero
knowledge cannot process stimuli either — a rock and a powered-off
phone are insentient precisely because they are also nescient.

Argument for treating them as distinct: an infant or an animal can be
nescient of advanced physics while still being highly sentient —
capable of joy, pain, warmth, fear. Conversely, a hypothetical AI
could hold near-total knowledge (near-zero nescience) while remaining
entirely insentient, lacking any subjective experience — knowing
everything, feeling nothing.
