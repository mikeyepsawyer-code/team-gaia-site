PRIMARY PASSAGE:

"Authenticity is the empath's first, least obvious discipline -- before
you can feel with someone else, you have to stop editing what you feel
yourself. Empathy is the skill this whole book is named for: the
antenna tuned outward, picking up a field that isn't yours. Affection
is what empathy becomes once it's been held long enough to warm --
feeling-with, given time, turns into feeling-for. And reciprocity is
the correction that keeps empathy from becoming exhaustion. An antenna
that only receives eventually burns out; the current has to run both
ways, or the empath simply drains. This is the whole arc of the book in
four words: tune in, receive, warm, and let it flow back."

ADDENDUM — EMPOWERED VS. UNINTEGRATED EMPATH (same session, Neuroticism
material identified as doing real work here too — distinguishing the
empowered empath from the empath who hasn't yet integrated their
sensitivity):

"There are two kinds of empath, and the difference is not sensitivity
-- both have it in full. The difference is whether the four words are
being run through Openness, Agreeableness, Extraversion, and
Conscientiousness, or through Neuroticism.

The empath who hasn't yet understood their superpower experiences their
own four words exactly as Neuroticism predicts. Authenticity curdles
into self-protective performance, because showing the real feeling once
cost them and they haven't unlearned the flinch. Empathy tips into
personal distress -- someone else's pain arrives undifferentiated from
their own, so 'empathic' quietly becomes a synonym for 'overwhelmed.'
Affection turns into anxious grasping, holding too tightly because the
warmth feels too precious to trust. Reciprocity becomes compulsive
ledger-keeping -- tracking every gift given and received because the
felt debt never gets to settle, only accumulate.

The empowered empath has the identical sensitivity and none of the
distortion. Authenticity is simply available, not defended. Empathy
stays empathy -- feeling with someone rather than flooded instead of
them. Affection is warmth given freely because it costs nothing to give
what you trust will return. Reciprocity settles, closes, doesn't need a
spreadsheet.

The work of this book was never to become more sensitive. You already
are. The work is moving from the first kind of empath to the second --
same antenna, different weather."
