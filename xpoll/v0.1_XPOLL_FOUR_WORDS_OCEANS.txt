"OCEAN doesn't just name this book's model -- it names what these four
words need from your psyche to work. Openness is what lets authenticity
out: the willingness to be surprised by your own feeling before you
edit it into something more presentable. Agreeableness is what carries
empathy: the trait already tuned to register someone else's state
before your own. Extraversion pushes affection outward into visible
warmth; its opposite banks the same warmth and shows it more quietly,
but it's the same current either way. Conscientiousness is what makes
reciprocity reliable instead of accidental -- the follow-through that
turns a felt debt into a settled one.

Neuroticism doesn't enable anything. It's what makes all four harder to
do honestly. High neuroticism turns authenticity into self-protective
performance -- too anxious to show the real feeling. It turns empathy
into personal distress -- someone else's pain becomes about managing
your own spike rather than attending to them. It turns affection into
anxious grasping instead of warmth freely given. It turns reciprocity
into compulsive ledger-keeping instead of settling. Four traits build
the instrument. The fifth is the headwind they're all working against."
