TEAM GAIA — MANUSCRIPT FORMATTING STANDARD
Established Aug 22, 2026. Applies catalog-wide to all interior
manuscript formatting for print/ebook publication.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
TYPOGRAPHY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

TITLE
  Font: Cinzel
  Size: 28-32pt
  Color: deep gold/copper (matches cover gold logic — dark bg gets
  light/pastel gold, light bg gets dark copper gold)
  Alignment: centered

SUBTITLE
  Font: Cinzel
  Size: 16-18pt
  Color: muted gold or charcoal
  Alignment: centered

QUOTE / EPIGRAPH
  Font: Cormorant Garamond Italic (Parisienne reserved for short
  standalone script moments — dedication pages, single-line epigraphs
  — not body-adjacent quote blocks, which need to stay readable)
  Size: 14-16pt
  Color: charcoal, slightly muted
  Alignment: centered

BODY TEXT
  Font: Cormorant Garamond (regular)
  Size: 11-11.5pt
  Color: near-black (not pure black)
  Alignment: justified, hyphenation on

CHAPTER OPENERS
  Drop cap on first letter of chapter's opening paragraph.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PAGE GEOMETRY (6×9 trim, matching cover production standard)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Outer margin: 0.75"
  Top margin: 0.75"
  Bottom margin: 0.75"
  Inner (gutter) margin: 0.875-1" (accounts for binding)
  Bleed safety: 0.125" from trim edge for any full-bleed elements
  Background: plain white or warm cream stock

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PAGE ELEMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PAGE NUMBERS
  Position: bottom center

TABLE OF CONTENTS
  Auto-generated from chapter/part headings, placed after title/
  copyright page, before opening material or Part I.

RUNNING HEADERS (mirrored, odd/even)
  Left leaf (verso): book title
  Right leaf (recto): current chapter title

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SCOPE OF WHAT GETS FORMATTED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Only settled manuscript content is formatted for publication —
title, subtitle, front matter, parts, chapters, body text. Editorial
scaffolding (CANDIDATE MATERIAL / XPOLL blocks, DRAFT NOTES, version
changelogs) stays in the GitHub-canonical working file but is
excluded from formatted publication drafts until placed.
