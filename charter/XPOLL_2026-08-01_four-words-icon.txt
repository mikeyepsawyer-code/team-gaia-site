--- LOBBY ---
Before the doors open, four words. Not virtues to practice -- a sequence to
fall through. Authenticity: stop performing, even for yourself. Empathy: let
someone else's field become visible. Affection: let the visibility warm you.
Reciprocity: let the warmth move both directions until you can't find the
seam. This is the lobby. Everything past this point assumes you've already
crossed it once.

--- AMORE ---
Ovid never used these words, but he built his seductions on their order.
Authenticity first -- the mask slips before anything real can pass between
two people. Then empathy, the labor of imagining her hunger as vividly as
your own. Affection follows empathy the way heat follows friction; it
cannot be summoned first. And reciprocity is the whole argument of the Ars
stripped of its cynicism: love that only flows one way isn't love, it's
harvesting.

--- SLOW THE FUCK DOWN ---
You cannot rush any of these. Authenticity takes as long as it takes to
stop lying to your own body. Empathy takes longer -- you have to actually
wait for another person's signal instead of assuming it. Affection arrives
on its own schedule, never yours. Reciprocity is the slowest of all,
because it requires two nervous systems finding the same tempo. Try to
speed through this list and you get its counterfeit: transaction wearing
intimacy's clothes.

--- OCEANS OF LOVE ---
The ocean doesn't perform its tides for an audience -- authenticity,
undisturbed. It doesn't wait to be struck before it moves toward the shore
-- empathy, the current already reaching for the coast before the coast
asks. Every wave that lands carries the warmth of a whole ocean behind it
-- affection, banked and released, never manufactured on the spot. And what
leaves the shore returns to the sea exactly as salt returns to itself --
reciprocity, the closed loop of water. Love, like the ocean, was never one
direction. It only looks that way from the beach.

--- RADIANT SENSITIVITY ---
Authenticity is the empath's first, least obvious discipline -- before you
can feel with someone else, you have to stop editing what you feel
yourself. Empathy is the skill this whole book is named for: the antenna
tuned outward, picking up a field that isn't yours. Affection is what
empathy becomes once it's been held long enough to warm -- feeling-with,
given time, turns into feeling-for. And reciprocity is the correction that
keeps empathy from becoming exhaustion. An antenna that only receives
eventually burns out; the current has to run both ways, or the empath
simply drains. This is the whole arc of the book in four words: tune in,
receive, warm, and let it flow back.

--- OCEANS OF LOVE (v2 -- ACTIVE DRAFT) ---
OCEAN doesn't just name this book's model -- it names what these four
words need from your psyche to work. Openness is what lets authenticity
out: the willingness to be surprised by your own feeling before you edit
it into something more presentable. Agreeableness is what carries empathy:
the trait already tuned to register someone else's state before your own.
Extraversion pushes affection outward into visible warmth; its opposite
banks the same warmth and shows it more quietly, but it's the same current
either way. Conscientiousness is what makes reciprocity reliable instead
of accidental -- the follow-through that turns a felt debt into a settled
one.

Neuroticism doesn't enable anything. It's what makes all four harder to do
honestly. High neuroticism turns authenticity into self-protective
performance -- too anxious to show the real feeling. It turns empathy into
personal distress -- someone else's pain becomes about managing your own
spike rather than attending to them. It turns affection into anxious
grasping instead of warmth freely given. It turns reciprocity into
compulsive ledger-keeping instead of settling. Four traits build the
instrument. The fifth is the headwind they're all working against.
