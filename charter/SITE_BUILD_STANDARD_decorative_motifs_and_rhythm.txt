SITE BUILD STANDARD — DECORATIVE MOTIF LANGUAGE & SPACING RHYTHM
Added August 25, 2026, after the fine-tooth-comb pass on index.html.

THE STAR MOTIF (&#10022;, ✦)
Established today as the site's flanking-decoration glyph, used two
ways at two different scales:
  - List-flanking (subtle): font-size: 0.6em of the text it flanks,
    color rgba(196,146,42,0.7), either side of a centered line, with
    &nbsp;&nbsp; spacing rather than a hard margin. Used in the
    Library Index list.
  - Section-title-flanking (bold): font-size clamp(1.4rem, 6vw, 2.4rem),
    color rgba(196,146,42,0.75), placed either side of a stacked
    two-line title inside a flex container (display:flex; align-
    items:center; justify-content:center; gap:14px) so the star sits
    vertically centered against the full two-line block, not against
    either line individually. Used on all four in-grid section titles.
Use this glyph, at the appropriate scale, anywhere a similar
flanking-decoration need comes up, rather than introducing a new
symbol — the diamond (already used in rule-flourish) and the star are
now the two established ornamental marks on this site. Don't add a
third without a reason.

SPACING RHYTHM: LIGHT WITHIN A GROUP, HEAVY BETWEEN GROUPS
The three hero eyebrow lines (Eclectic philosophy / Empathic
psychology / Luminous sexuality) used to have a full rule-flourish
(diamond, ~400px wide) between each one — visually, that made three
closely-related words read as three separate sections. Fixed by
snuggling them together (minimal margin, no flourish between them)
and moving the one heavy diamond rule-flourish to AFTER the third
line, where it actually does the job of separating the group from
what comes next (the author bio block).

The general principle, worth applying anywhere a cluster of related
short items sits next to a heavier document structure: items within
the same conceptual group get little or no visual separation (a thin
1px line at most, or just spacing). The full rule-flourish is reserved
for boundaries BETWEEN groups. Using the same heavy divider between
every item flattens the hierarchy and makes a tight cluster read as
loose and disconnected.

PULL-QUOTE PLACEMENT
The "Ecstasy is not hedonism..." quote sat, by what looks like
historical accident of build order, at the very end of the adult
section block — after the section title, after the ADULT TITLES
videos, after the age-gate text. Moving it up to sit directly under
the Somatic Ecstasy title (before the videos) put it next to the
content it's actually doing rhetorical work for.
General rule: when reorganizing or reviewing an existing page, check
whether supporting quotes/pull-text are still adjacent to the content
they're meant to support, rather than assuming their current position
is intentional. Build order and narrative order aren't the same thing,
and content that accumulates over several sessions is especially
likely to have drifted.
