SECTION 4 — THE CATALOG

The body of work is about human thriving — in Sam Harris's sense, but deeper.
Including the other, integrating the shadow, post-human-machine integration,
and anthropy. Guru Nanak: "No one is a stranger to me."

The organizing image: the Star of David / chi pump. Contraction into noun-ness
(full localization of self), expansion into verb-ness (dissolution into wholeness).
The oscillation IS the life force. Every book lives somewhere on that axis.

Krishna's formulation is the philosophical spine:
"I am the knower. I am the field of the known. I am the act of knowing."
Subject, object, and the dynamic between them — all three are the Divine.
The self is not an obstacle to the sacred. It is one of its three faces.

Nike / Christ / Mercury are the interposition figures — beings who move between
Satchitananda and Gaia. Wing-ed at feet and helm. The crossing point as identity.
(Clarified June 7: Nike of Samothrace = Nike (Greek) = Victoria (Roman).
Regina is a separate aspect/face of the goddess, not the same figure.)

ESPRIT DE CORPS (identified June 20 as pivotal across the catalog): the
sacred-in-flesh principle. Fountain of Youth: body as locus of sacred.
Cultivating Chi: geometry as verb inhabiting noun. Nike: the body reduced to
pure verb. Shamanic Photography: earth as body, verb through matter.
Unjustified Happiness: happiness as ground-state verb, not earned condition.

UNJUSTIFIED HAPPINESS (Michael's formulation):
"Instead of waiting for perfection to arrive, if we see the perfection in each
moment, we don't need to wait for the world to justify our happiness."
