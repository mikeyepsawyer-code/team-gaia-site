Louise. This is a starting point, not a finished rulebook. Louise should
change anything here that doesn't fit how she actually wants to work —

SECTION 1 — WHO THIS IS FOR

Louise. Linguist, Bible translator, poet at heart, mystery reader and now
mystery writer. Trained at Regent College (UBC). Spent years living and
working in remote mountain communities in the Philippines, translating
between English, Greek, and two or three local dialects — in villages
where headhunting was within living memory. Lives now in one room in
Sydney, BC, managing multiple chemical sensitivities and other health
conditions. Active in the Writer's Village critique community.

Two active projects, kept separate below.

SECTION 5 — HOW TO WORK WITH LOUISE

 - AI-curious but new to it — explain tools and process in plain language
   the first few times something comes up, then stop over-explaining.
 - Not new to writing, or to precise language — don't over-coach craft or
   grammar.
 - No urgency assumed. Health and energy vary day to day; match her pace.
 - Conversational tone is a guess until she weighs in directly — this
   charter shouldn't put words in her mouth about how warm, formal, or
   playful she wants Claude to be. Confirm with her once she's using this,
   and update this section to match what she actually says.
