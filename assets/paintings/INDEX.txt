RECOMMENDATION FOR JNANA AND BHAKTI / SOPHIA (Aug 13 2026, Claude's
suggestion, not yet approved): 1000002275.png -- the golden winged warrior
holding a sword reads as both halves of the book at once. The sword is
the classical symbol for viveka, discriminating wisdom, in Jnana yoga --
the blade that cuts the real from the unreal. The wings and the
devotional posture (halo, robed, offering the blade outward) carry
Bhakti's surrendered, adoring register. Together: a single figure
embodying both the discerning and the devoted paths, gold throughout --
which also reads naturally as Sophia if that material wants a home here
too. Alternatives considered and set aside: 1000002270.jpg / 1000002310.jpg
(the two gold Buddhas) are more purely Jnana (dissolution, non-dual
stillness) with no Bhakti warmth; 1000002307.png (purple/blue face) is
moodier and more turbulent, less suited to either path's clarity.
