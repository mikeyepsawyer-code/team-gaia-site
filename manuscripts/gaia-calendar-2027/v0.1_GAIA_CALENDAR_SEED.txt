── OPENING MATERIAL ──

[MICHAEL — verbatim, Aug 20 2026, originally drafted for Sacred in
Carnate's priest/priestess couplet, reassigned here]

I am your priest, you are MY GODDESS —
we consecrate each other in the same breath.

[CLAUDE — draft, structural note] This lands differently here than it
would have in Sacred in Carnate. There, priest/priestess was a
mutual, symmetrical pairing. Here, addressed to Gaia specifically,
"my Goddess" makes the addressee the Earth herself — the whole book
can be read as one long address to her, which is a strong opening
gesture for a calendar built around her portrait, month by month.
