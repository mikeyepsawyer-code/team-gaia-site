MENTAL HEALTH AND PERSONALITY STRUCTURE

MEDICATION

MAOIs remain an absolute no. High-dose SSRIs or other serotonergic and dopaminergic medications warrant your prescriber's awareness, particularly at the sustained intensities this book teaches — you are, after all, in the business of triggering large endogenous releases of exactly those same chemical families.

NEURODIVERGENT PRACTITIONERS AND PARTNERS

TECHNIQUE: SINGING THE BOWL

"A struck bell is loudest at the first instant and dies from there. A
rim-caressed bowl is the opposite instrument: it starts from silence
and builds. The tone rises only if the hand refuses to hurry, keeps
unbroken contact, and circles at the speed the bowl asks for rather
than the speed the hand prefers. Press too hard and it chokes. Move too
fast and it stutters. Stay slow, stay continuous, and the whole vessel
begins to sing from everywhere at once.

This is the book's entire argument, holdable in one hand. Percussive
touch gets an immediate loud response that immediately fades.
Sustained, slow, circumferential contact builds resonance that does not
fade, because it is being continuously fed. The body is the bowl.
Speed is the strike. Slowness is the rim. A partner's body approached
percussively flinches and peaks and is finished. The same body,
circled patiently, unbroken contact, no destination, begins after some
minutes to hum from everywhere at once. Nothing was struck. Everything
is singing.

Practice notes: begin literally, with an actual singing bowl if one is
in the house, to teach the hand what sustained coaxing feels like. Then
translate: same pressure discipline, same continuity, same surrender of
tempo to the instrument. The measure of success is not intensity but
duration of resonance."

Candidate placement: this is broader than Ch4's labia-specific
application -- worth its own short section or technique callout, likely
in Part I or as a bridge into Part II, since it generalizes the
principle across the whole body rather than one structure.
