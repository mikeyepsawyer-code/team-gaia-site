OPENING MATERIAL

[CLAUDE — draft, structural note] Reciprocal version of the line
placed in Gaia Calendar 2027 — there it addresses Gaia directly as
"my Goddess" (singular, external); here both people carry the
divine internally and consecrate each other as an act between equals.
Fits this book's register: the sacred takes place in an ordinary,
present-tense body, not somewhere else.
