ANGLE FOR KINTSUGI:
The crack is the memoir's darkest page; the gold seam is the valve
reopened at the break. Everyone walks in their own personal hell until
they recognize it is also heaven: same address, different pressure.
Suffering as felt resistance that teaches — the crack as curriculum,
the repair as recognition, not erasure. Openness does not erase the
body (or the break); it fulfills it. Gold = radiance received at the
exact site of the clench.
