# Scribe Companion Files

Short, standing-instruction files meant to be handed to an AI assistant
(this one or another) before or during a creative/technical build. Each
file is a self-contained principle — upload the ones relevant to your
project, not the whole set.

These are different from the rest of Scribe: Scribe itself is written
to be *read*. These files are written to be *followed* — instructions
for the assistant, not narrative for the person.

Updated as part of a weekly review of what actually got re-used across
more than one build. A lesson doesn't make it here on its first
appearance — only once it's shown up more than once, or is clearly a
principle rather than a tool-specific quirk.

## Files

- `measurement-proportional-vs-absolute.md` — decide, before doing any
  layout/sizing math, which measurements must scale with the container
  and which must stay a fixed real size. Conflating the two is a
  common source of "it looked right in one preview and wrong
  everywhere else."
- `solving-for-shared-targets.md` — when two separately-built pieces
  need to meet at a point, solve for that point directly from the
  confirmed target, and confirm what the target actually is first. An
  approximation can look exact and still be aimed at the wrong thing.
